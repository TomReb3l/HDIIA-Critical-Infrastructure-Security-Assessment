# Guard Force Performance Review
## October service decision for a fictional data centre

**Portfolio simulation · Thomas Marinidis · November 2026**  
All records and contract terms below were created for this exercise. The case assumes a site security manager reviews a guarding supplier's monthly report before accepting service delivery.

### Decision requested

Accept October coverage as meeting the monthly hours target. Keep two corrective actions open for incident escalation and verification of completed work. Ask the supplier's contract manager for a recovery plan within five working days. The sample contract contains no service-credit formula, so this review makes no deduction recommendation.

### Terms and source records

The exercise covers one continuous post for 31 days, measured in UTC. The contract sets three independent conditions. Coverage must reach 99.5% of scheduled hours. Every priority incident must be escalated within 15 minutes of detection. Every corrective action due in the month needs dated closure evidence by its due date.

| Coverage period | Scheduled hours | Verified staffed hours |
|---|---:|---:|

The supplier counted three hours of relief coverage. The signed relief records do not establish those hours. Record them as unverified. Actual absence during those hours is undetermined.

### Findings and follow-up

| Measure | Result | Management response |
|---|---|---|
| Verified coverage | 741 / 744 = 99.60%. Target met. Supplier reported 100%. | Site supervisor to reconcile three disputed hours within five working days. Keep the original evidence cut-off visible in any revised score. |
| Timely escalations | 2 / 3 = 66.7%. P2 exceeded the limit by six minutes. P3 met it exactly. | Supplier to review P2, establish the cause and assign an action owner. Site security to review the next three priority escalations. |
| Evidenced closure by due date | 2 / 3 = 66.7%. A2 remains unverified. | Request the dated attendance record. If unavailable, repeat and document the briefing. Record its actual completion date. |

**Why the measures stay separate.** High coverage cannot offset a missed incident deadline. Likewise, a missing signature can limit assurance without establishing that a post was vacant. Each finding warrants a different management response.

### Interview question

The supplier offers a newly signed statement for all three relief hours. What would change your decision? Check whether the statement is supported by contemporaneous, attributable records. Update coverage if justified, retain the original review and assess incident escalation separately.
